Brand Overview

Limit Break is a competitive gaming tournament brand based in Cyprus — operating at the intersection of community, competition, and culture.

From Super Smash Bros. to multi-game events, Limit Break creates spaces where players push past their limits. The brand draws on retro arcade aesthetics and modern esports energy to deliver an experience that feels both nostalgic and electric.

Every asset, every event, every graphic should feel like it belongs in an arcade cabinet from the future.

Logo

Primary Logo

The Limit Break wordmark is the brand's most important asset. The stacked letterforms feature a bold, condensed style with a heavy black stroke — "LIMIT" sits on top in electric cyan, "BREAK" follows below in a sweep from cyan-blue to vivid magenta. Never separate the two words.

On white / light backgrounds

On dark backgrounds

Logo with Tagline

In most digital contexts, pair the logo with the "Gaming Tournaments" tagline set in spaced uppercase below the mark. This combination appears in banners, thumbnails, and broadcast overlays.

Clear Space

Always preserve breathing room around the logo. The minimum clear space on all sides equals the height of the "L" in LIMIT. Never crowd the mark with competing elements.

✕ Distort or stretch the proportions

✕ Rotate or skew the mark

Brand Gradient Spectrum

#00D4FF Electric Cyan
#0055FF Deep Blue
#7700FF Neon Violet
#CC00EE Deep Magenta

The full spectrum spans five stops: Electric Cyan → Deep Blue → Neon Violet → Deep Magenta → Neon Magenta. Use the two-stop shorthand (Cyan → Magenta) at 135° for most applications. The five-stop version adds depth to large-format backgrounds, hero frames, and broadcast overlays. Always run the gradient left-to-right or top-to-bottom — never reverse it.

Accent Colors

Color Usage Rules

Do

  • Use Electric Cyan and Neon Magenta as the primary accent pair
  • Apply the full gradient to borders, frames, and hero dividers
  • Layer backgrounds Void → Deep Navy → Dark Surface → Elevated Surface
  • Use Pure White for headings and Off-White for body copy
  • Use tints for backgrounds, fills, and glow surfaces — not for text
  • Reserve Champion Gold strictly for tournament results and trophies

Don't

  • Fill large areas with full-saturation neons — they're highlights, not fills
  • Place cyan and magenta directly side-by-side without a dark buffer
  • Use any brand gradient on white or light backgrounds
  • Reverse the gradient direction (Magenta → Cyan)
  • Use Active Jade or Champion Gold as primary or decorative colors
  • Introduce off-brand colors (orange, red, brown) without explicit justification

Typography

The Limit Break type system pairs a bold, condensed display typeface with a clean versatile sans-serif for all supporting copy. The display weight delivers maximum arcade energy at large sizes; the body face ensures legibility at every other scale.

Display — Barlow Condensed

Used for event titles, section headings, schedules, and all large-format text. The condensed width accommodates long strings at high point sizes without wrapping. Always set at ExtraBold (800) or Black (900) weight, in uppercase.

Black 900 ExtraBold 800 Bold 700 (labels only)

Visual Language

The Limit Break visual language is defined by neon light on darkness. Thin glowing borders, gradient frames, subtle grid textures, and deep atmospheric backgrounds combine to produce a coherent retro-arcade-meets-esports aesthetic.

Borders & Frames

Neon borders are a defining element across all Limit Break assets. Use gradient borders (cyan → magenta) to frame key content. Borders should be 2 – 4px, always on dark backgrounds, and kept crisp and geometric.

Gradient Border

Cyan → Magenta · 135°

Glow Effects

Glow is used sparingly to suggest neon light. Apply via box-shadow or text-shadow in brand colors. A good glow reads as ambient light, not blur — keep opacity low and radius generous.

Cyan Glow

0 0 24px rgba(0,212,255,0.5)

0 0 24px rgba(238,0,204,0.5)

06

Brand Applications

The following examples show how the Limit Break brand is applied across real digital assets. Each piece maintains the core elements: dark background, gradient borders, bold condensed typography, and consistent logo placement.

Event Schedule

Portrait format with a gradient border (cyan → magenta) framing the full asset. Logo anchors the top. Day headings in Barlow Condensed, time/location in smaller Inter. Dark blurred game scene as background texture.

  • Format: Portrait · 1080 × 1920px
  • Border: Gradient frame, 3–4px
  • Heading font: Barlow Condensed 800

Platform Banner

Widescreen banner for start.gg and social media headers. Logo is centered on an atmospheric dark background. Gradient border frames the full composition. Partner logos sit in the corners at reduced opacity.

  • Format: Landscape · 2560 × 540px
  • Logo: Centered, with tagline
  • Border: Gradient frame

Video Thumbnail

Dark cinematic background with centered logo + "Gaming Tournaments" tagline. Used for YouTube and streaming platform presence. Minimal text — the logo carries the asset.

  • Format: 16:9 · 1280 × 720px
  • Logo: Large, centered, with glow
  • Background: Blurred game scene

Player Announcement

Landscape format announcing individual attendees. Player name in Barlow Condensed dominates the layout. Character art or imagery on the left. Logo mark in top corner. Date and event detail in white Inter below.

  • Format: Landscape · 1200 × 400px
  • Name: Barlow Condensed 900
  • Logo: Corner placement, small

Broadcast Overlay (BRB)

Full-screen overlay for use during stream holds. Dark vignette background with blurred game footage. Logo centered at mid-frame. "WE'LL BE RIGHT BACK!" in bold white Barlow Condensed below the mark. Partner logo in corner.

  • Format: 16:9 · 1920 × 1080px
  • Text: Barlow Condensed 800, white
  • Background: Dark vignette overlay
